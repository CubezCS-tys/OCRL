Correct the draft labels

How this works
  • Every line Azure found has a draft label from our structure model. A thick top edge marks where a block begins. A dashed box or a "?" means the model was unsure.
  • Click a line (on the page or in the list). Shift-click selects a run of lines. Arrow keys move up and down.
  • Press a type's letter, or click its button, to relabel the selected lines.
  • Press B to switch "a block begins here" on or off for the selected line. Press M to make the selected lines one block.
  • When the page is right, press Enter. Your work is kept in this browser as you go; "Save my corrections to a file" writes it out.
