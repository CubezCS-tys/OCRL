How the parser's models fit together

A scanned page goes in and a typed, ordered document comes out, with no outside service. Three small trained models do the work, with rules between them. The structure model is the weak part, and it is taught by other models rather than by people. The purple dashed boxes are places a vision-language model such as Qwen could be added. None of them is built yet.

Known gaps today

  • Tables are read column by column, not as rows and cells. Idea D aims here.
  • English pages are put in order as if they were Arabic (columns right to left).
  • Vowelled text, handwriting and filled forms are not handled. Idea C would at least send such documents down another route.

Pending, not measured yet: the longer and bigger runs of the structure model with an eye, and Qwen with no training on the 70 judged pages. Idea A's speed of 1 to 3 seconds a page is an estimate.
