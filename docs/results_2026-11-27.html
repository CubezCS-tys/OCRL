Where the local parser stands

27 November 2026, after the first long working night. A scanned page now goes in and a typed, ordered document comes out with no Azure and no Gemini in the loop: a trained line finder, a line reader, and a structure model, on top of the ink-blob layout. This page records what was measured, on what, and what is still weak.

What was wrong, and is fixed

  • Pages were drawn by the wrong box. Azure measures the visible page (the crop box); we drew the media box, misplacing every line on 45% of documents. All earlier line numbers were redone.
  • Photographs were read as ink. They are now found by their grey tones and painted out before any blob is made.
  • Table frames were one blob that swallowed every cell. Ruled lines are removed; a rule standing alone still cuts the page into regions.
  • The same ink was read twice where a column and a wider footer were joined into one region that overlapped the next column.
  • Small marks joined the wrong line when two lines shared a height (two columns, a table row). Marks far from any letter are dropped as margin specks.
  • Latin and mixed lines came out in the wrong order from the mirrored line reader.
  • List items were called paragraphs. A rule now reads lists off the page: an item opens with a mark and runs to the next mark, a gap, or a line that stops short.

What is still weak

  • Tables are found cell by cell but read column by column, not row by row.
  • English and mixed pages are ordered right to left like Arabic ones.
  • Magazine layouts: the structure model has never seen pull quotes or standfirsts.
  • Vowelled text, handwriting, forms are out of scope so far.
  • Truth to develop against: three hand-keyed documents is too few. A first census of 74,947 pages from 1,520 journals (made from Azure's boxes alone) groups pages into 14 kinds; about a fifth of pages are kinds the parser is weak on. Seventy pages, five of each kind, are drafted and waiting to be corrected.

Cost

Rented GPU time for everything above: 6.23 dollars. One machine was deleted mid-run by a second safety guard on the same account, losing a trained line finder and the downloads; results are now copied back after every job.
